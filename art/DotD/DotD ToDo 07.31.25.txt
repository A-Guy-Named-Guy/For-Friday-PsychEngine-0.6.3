				- Enemy mixups
					- Watch carefully for the enemy shaking your offense
					- Defend, and watch their guard
					- If no guard arrow is active, they cannot block
				- Basic attack chains
					- Your attack's properties differ depending on their sequence
					- Your first attack deals strong damage, but is only in play on countering
					- Your second attack uses little stamina, with decent damage
					- Your third attack is strong and executes instantly
					- By aiming away from your opponent's guard initially, you can set up to use the third attack

Gameplay Design
	- Non-sing combat
		- Enemy Behavior
			- Behavior can no longer be charted
				- Need to develop an "AI" for how the enemy acts and responds
			- Enemy Attacks
				- Enemy attacks in "Chains", or combos
				- Attack direction typically set, or can vary on a specific move
				- Special properties on different steps in the chain
					- Unblockable, uninterruptible, bash, etc.
				- Chains can chain to other movesets until interrupted
			- Disruption
				- Making a correct move that forces opponent out of their chain
					- Superior block
						- Ends chain, but much stricter and specific to pull off
							- Requires either a slower enemy attack or a hard-read
						- Some attacks opponent can defend this maneuver
					- Parry
						- Defends but doesn't end chain for most attacks
						- Some "Chain Finishers" have a special parry that signals a forced chain-end
					- Bash
						- Very slow, rarely can interrupt mid chain
						- Works great on chain end
			- Defense
				- Certain attacks and opponent actions restrict defensive actions
					- In a neutral state, the opponent defends against everything
				- Blocking
					- Opponent switches their guard after every interaction
						- Enemy blocking or getting hit, etc.
				- Beat/Bind
					- Attacking the same direction as the opponent's guard keeps their guard in place
						- Thus, match direction attack -> different direction = bypass guard
					- Primarily used to deal health damage
					- Opponent can randomly counterattack when performing this action
						- There's enough time to react, but it requires paying attention
				- Posture/Health
					- Posture fills faster than health lowers
					- Lower health leads to lower posture recovery
					- Opponent-specific defenses and health values can tilt best victory one way or another
						
	- Sing combat
		- Feint notes
			- Two attacks displayed at once
				- "Feint" note missing a detail
					- Maybe the white shine?
	- Both
		- Special: Offensive Stance
			- Adopts a guard that alters a lot of effects
				- Heavy attack
					- Attacks gain new properties
				- Superior block
					- Parry attempts instead overpower with an attack
					- Some opponent attacks will defend this move
				- Feint
					- Adopting an offensive stance prompts opponent to react
				- Bash
					- Pressing "down" to dodge makes an attack attempt perform a bash
			- Light Attack
				- An attack performed WITHOUT offensive stance
					- Vanilla attack
					- Laid out here to figure out tools of a normal light
				- Parries
					- Parries increase enemy posture
				- Interrupt
					- Certain enemy actions can be interrupted
				- Punish
					- Certain player actions return to neutral and use the default light
		- Interruption/Hyperarmor
			- Actions being interrupted is not yet a feature
